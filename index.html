The biggest, fastest-growing market is the deep ocean

While space boosts the company's tech credibility, the deep ocean is Infinity Fuel’s biggest, fastest-growing market.

Enter the world of Uncrewed Underwater Vehicles (UUVs).

UUV’s are autonomous underwater drones. The US Navy uses them for mine detection, reconnaissance, surveillance and mapping the ocean floor. Commercial sectors use UUV’s for offshore oil and gas, subsea pipeline inspection and ocean research.

Underwater drones have struggled with a major issue: battery life. They currently only last 20-40 hours of use. After that, they need a costly retrieval and recharge.
